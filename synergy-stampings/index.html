What do you stamp, exactly?

Precision metal stampings and pressed components: body and chassis brackets, reinforcements, shafts and linkage assemblies, panel parts, core stampings and general stampings to drawing. If it is cut and pressed from sheet, it fits our line.

Can you take a part from design to mass production?

Yes. Our flow is process design, tool tryout, mass production and delivery management, all in one plant at F-133, MIDC Ambad, Nashik. Tooling is built with the group's in-house tool room, so tryout corrections happen on our floor, not over email.

How fast do you answer an RFQ?

Within 24 hours of your drawing. Send STEP, DXF, DWG or PDF through the quote form, and an engineer replies with a price or specific questions. Tick the NDA box and we sign before opening the file.

Who do you already supply?

Through the SCF Group, our parts reach automotive and electrical OEM programs. The group is an approved source for Mahindra & Mahindra and supplies ABB India, BHEL, Gabriel India, Lauritz Knudsen, KPIT, Sharda Motors and Sipra Engineers.

What about finish and assembly?

Welding, plating coordination and powder coating are handled inside the group, so a stamped part can arrive finished and assembled, not as a bare blank you still have to manage.

What volumes do you run?

Prototype, pilot and volume runs all go through the same tool room. The group grew from Rs 21.50 Cr to Rs 52.26 Cr in four years on repeat OEM orders, so volume programs are the norm, not the exception.

What materials can you stamp?

Sheet metal programs: CRCA, mild steel, stainless and coated steels in the thicknesses our 40 to 300 tonne presses handle. Name the material on your drawing and we confirm the press fit in the quote.

Where is the plant?

F-133, MIDC Ambad, Nashik, Maharashtra 422010. Call +91 97637 10376 to schedule a plant visit and see the press line running.
