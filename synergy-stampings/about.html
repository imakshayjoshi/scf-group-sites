The stampings arm of SCF Group

Synergy Stampings runs the press line at F-133, MIDC Ambad, Nashik. Behind it sits a group that has manufactured in Nashik since 2002 and grew from Rs 21.5 Cr to Rs 52.26 Cr in four years.

What a group backing means for a buyer

Your stamping program sits next to a tool room, a fabrication shop, a powder coating line and a panel shop. When a part needs one more operation, it happens across the yard, not across the state.

The group mission is plain: total techno solutions with minimum lead time, at competitive price and quality, for the automotive and electrical sectors.

Presses you can come and watch

Buyers are welcome at the plant. Walk the tool room, watch a tryout, and meet the people who will run your part.

Quality is a written target

Group quality objectives: on-time delivery, 85% overall equipment effectiveness, zero customer complaints. Reviewed monthly, tied to how we schedule your press time.
