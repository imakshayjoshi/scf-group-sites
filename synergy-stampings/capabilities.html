The right tonnage for the part

Mechanical presses from 40 to 300 tonnes cover thin-gauge brackets to heavy stampings. Material and thickness on your drawing decide the press; the RFQ form asks for exactly that.

Progressive and single-hit tooling both run here, planned at process design stage.

Finish and assembly through the group

Stampings often need welding, plating or powder coating before they are useful. Those stages run inside the SCF Group, at the same address, so your stamped part can arrive finished and assembled.

Welded assemblies

Stamped arms and brackets welded to shafts and plates, built to drawing.

Powder coating

Group coating line with conveyor and booth, so finish and dimension share one owner.

Panel assembly

Pressed panel parts move straight into fabricated, wired control panel assemblies.
