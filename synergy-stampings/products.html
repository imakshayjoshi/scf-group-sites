Selector and breaker shafts — running in OEM programs today.

Selector and breaker shafts

Shaft and linkage assemblies with stamped arms and fingers, welded and finished in zinc or black. Built for gear selector mechanisms and electrical breaker mechanisms where every variant must match the approved sample.

  • Gear selector and shift shafts
  • Electrical breaker shafts
  • Zinc and black finishes

Typical buyer: automotive and switchgear sourcing teams who need every variant to match the approved sample.

Stamped brackets and mounting plates

Punched, bent and fitted assemblies: mounting plates, brackets, slide rails and hardware-fitted stampings. One plant stamps and assembles, so the part arrives complete.

  • Body and chassis brackets
  • Spring-fitted assemblies
  • Reinforcements and frame parts

Typical buyer: body-in-white and chassis programs consolidating stamped and fitted parts with one vendor.

Core stampings and press components

Core stampings, side assemblies and side plates for electrical programs, pressed on 40 to 300 tonne presses with group-built dies.

  • Core stampings and side plates
  • In-house die build and tryout
  • Prototype to volume runs

Typical buyer: electrical programs needing core stampings and side assemblies at repeat volume.

Shock absorber components

Outer tubes and pressed components for suspension programs, with clevis and eye-mount variants, finished in black or left in plated steel.

  • Outer tubes and damper bodies
  • Clevis and eye mount variants
  • Coating through the group line

Typical buyer: suspension Tier-1s who want the part and the finish from one address.
