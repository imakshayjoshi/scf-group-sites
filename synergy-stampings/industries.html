Three industries. One line.

Automotive, switchgear and energy. Different drawings, same discipline: first article approved, then batch after batch that matches it.

Automotive Tier-1

Pressed and welded parts for vehicle programs: brackets, shafts, suspension components and reinforcements. The group is an approved source for Mahindra & Mahindra and supplies Gabriel India and Sharda Motors.

  • Shock absorber components
  • Selector shafts and linkages
  • Chassis brackets and reinforcements

Switchgear and electrical

Breaker mechanisms and panel hardware for switchgear OEMs. ABB India, BHEL and Lauritz Knudsen buy from the group.

  • Breaker shafts and mechanisms
  • Core stampings and side plates
  • Panel parts and assemblies

Energy and industrial

Pressed components for energy and industrial equipment programs, backed by group work on hydrogen fuel cell plates and EV development.

  • Pressed plates and frames
  • Prototype to pilot runs
  • NDA-first drawing exchange
