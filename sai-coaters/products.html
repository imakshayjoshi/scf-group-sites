Hydrogen fuel cell plates

Mono-polar and bi-polar plates with pressed flow-field channels for hydrogen fuel cell and clean energy programs. Our project development team works on hydrogen and EV programs, so prototype-to-pilot conversations start with people who already speak the language.

  • Mono-polar and bi-polar geometries
  • Pressed flow channels, batch repeatability
  • NDA signed before we open your file

Typical buyer: hydrogen and EV program engineers moving from prototype to pilot production.

Electrical breaker shafts

Welded breaker shaft assemblies for switchgear OEMs, in zinc-plated and black finishes. A standing production line for buyers who need the same shaft, batch after batch, without tolerance drift.

  • Welded assemblies to drawing
  • Zinc, chromate and black finishes
  • Repeat batches held to the same tool

Typical buyer: switchgear OEM sourcing teams dual-sourcing breaker mechanisms.

Press components and core stampings

Side assemblies, core stampings, side plates and pressed components for electrical and automotive programs. Stamped on our 40 to 300 tonne presses with dies from our own tool room.

  • Core stamping and side assemblies
  • In-house die build and maintenance
  • Prototype to volume

Typical buyer: electrical and automotive programs needing stamped parts at volume.

Control panels and base frames

Panel boxes, enclosures and base frames fabricated, powder coated, wired and assembled in one plant. Switchgear and automation buyers get a complete unit with one delivery date.

  • Fabrication, coating and wiring together
  • Distribution boxes and panel enclosures
  • Complete units, inspection ready

Typical buyer: switchgear and automation OEMs consolidating three vendors into one.

Shock absorber components

Outer tubes and pressed components for suspension programs, finished as coated parts on our in-house powder coating line. Part and finish from one vendor.

  • Shock absorber outer tubes
  • Powder coating in-house
  • Locomotive components also in production

Typical buyer: suspension Tier-1s and railway program sourcing heads.
