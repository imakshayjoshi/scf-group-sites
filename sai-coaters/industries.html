Four industries. One stack.

Automotive, electrical, railways and hydrogen energy. Different buyers, same need: one vendor who cuts, presses, welds and coats without drama.

Automotive

Tier-1 sourcing teams buy pressed and welded parts from us for repeat programs. Mahindra & Mahindra lists us as an approved source; Gabriel India and Sharda Motors buy from the group.

  • Shock absorber components
  • Pressed and welded assemblies
  • OTD as a stated quality objective

Electrical and switchgear

Switchgear OEMs source breaker shafts, panel enclosures and wired control panels. ABB India, BHEL and Lauritz Knudsen are on our customer list.

  • Breaker shafts and mechanisms
  • Control panels, wired and assembled
  • Core stampings and side assemblies

Railways

Locomotive components pressed and fabricated to drawing for railway programs, with the same tool room discipline our automotive buyers get.

  • Locomotive components
  • Fabricated frames and brackets
  • Powder-coated finish in-house

Hydrogen and clean energy

Energy teams come to us for mono-polar and bi-polar plates, with a project development manager dedicated to hydrogen and EV programs.

  • Fuel cell plates, prototype to pilot
  • NDA-first drawing exchange
  • Galvanised filters for GDC and LPDC plants

Whatever your industry, the process is the same: send a drawing, get a 24-hour quote from an engineer.
