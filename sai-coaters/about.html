The SCF Group, since 2002

Sai Coaters & Fabricators is the full-stack manufacturing arm of the SCF Group of companies, Nashik. Four units, two plants in MIDC Ambad, and a growth run from Rs 21.50 Cr to Rs 52.26 Cr in four years.

What the group stands on

Modern production facilities, professional management, a skilled shop floor, and a simple promise: total techno solutions with minimum lead time, at competitive price and quality, for the automotive and electrical sectors.

The group builds automobile components, electrical components for switchgear companies, SPMs for auto and electrical industries, turnkey paint shop and powder coating plants, and galvanised filters for GDC and LPDC.
