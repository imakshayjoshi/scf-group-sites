What buyers ask first

Something more specific on your drawing? Call +91 97637 10376 and an engineer answers either way.

Can you take a part from drawing to coated finish?

Yes. Tool room, laser cutting, press shop, fabrication and powder coating are all in-house across our two Nashik plants. One vendor owns the part from raw sheet to finished coating, and you get one quote and one delivery date.

What press range do you run?

Mechanical presses from 40 to 300 tonnes, including a SEYI SNX-110, with an in-house tool room that builds and maintains the dies. Tell us the material and thickness on your drawing and we will confirm the press fit in the quote.

How fast do you respond to an RFQ?

Within 24 hours of receiving your drawing, an engineer replies with questions or a price. Send STEP, DXF, DWG or PDF. If you need an NDA before we open the file, tick the box on the form and we sign first.

Are you approved by any OEMs?

Yes. Sai Coaters and Fabricators is an approved source for Mahindra & Mahindra. The group also supplies ABB India, BHEL, Gabriel India, Lauritz Knudsen, KPIT, Sharda Motors and Sipra Engineers.

What volumes can you handle?

The group grew from Rs 21.50 Cr to Rs 52.26 Cr in four years by taking repeat production for automotive and electrical OEMs. Prototype to pilot to volume runs are all quoted; annual volume on the RFQ form tells us which press and line to plan around.

Do you take small batches or only volume?

Both. Prototype and pilot batches run through the same tool room as volume work, so a good part can scale without changing vendor. Tell us the annual volume on the form and we plan the press and the die around it.

What materials do you work with?

Sheet metal programs are our daily work: CRCA, mild steel, stainless and coated steels in the thicknesses our 40 to 300 tonne presses handle. If your drawing names the material, we confirm the process fit in the quote.

Where are you located?

Two plants in MIDC Ambad, Nashik, Maharashtra 422010: F-133 and G-37. Buyers are welcome to visit the plant; call +91 97637 10376 to schedule a walk-through.
