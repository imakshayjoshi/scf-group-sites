Every process. One roof.

Every stage your part needs is inside our two plants in MIDC Ambad, Nashik. One quote covers cut, press, weld and coat. You never manage a chain of job shops again.

Tool room

Dies, press tools and fixtures are designed, built and maintained on our own floor. When a tool needs a correction at 7pm before a morning dispatch, the people who built it are the people who fix it.

Why buyers care

Outsourced tooling is where repeat orders drift. Dimensions move, and every vendor blames the last one. Our tool room keeps your die under the same roof as the press that runs it.

Tool maintenance history stays with us across years of repeat batches, so part ten thousand matches part one.

Laser cutting and laser welding

Profiles straight from your file

Send DXF or STEP and we cut nested profiles directly. Laser welding joins thin-gauge assemblies with low distortion, which matters when the part still has to pass through a coating line and arrive true.

Turret punching and bending cover high-repeat hole patterns and formed brackets.

Press shop, 40 to 300 tonnes

Mechanical presses from 40 to 300 tonnes, including a SEYI SNX-110, run stamped and pressed parts from prototype batches to volume programs.

Tonnage matched to the part

Tell us the material, thickness and annual volume on the RFQ form. We plan the press and the die around the part, then hold the same settings for every repeat batch.

Fabrication

Assemblies built to drawing

Cut, formed, welded and assembled frames, brackets and enclosures. Base frames, panel structures and welded assemblies ship as complete, inspection-ready units.

Welding assemblies for switchgear and suspension programs are a standing line, not an experiment.

Powder coating

Finish applied where the part is made

A conveyorised powder coating line with spray booth finishes the parts we press and fabricate. One owner for dimension and finish means no shipping bare parts across town and no finish disputes between vendors.

We also take coating-only job work for local fabricators, and we build turnkey paint shop and powder coating plants for buyers setting up their own lines.
